1.  An infusion of an invented compound is stopped and its plasma level is measured.

+-------------------------+----------------------+
| Time after infusion (h) | Plasma level (μg/mL) |
+=========================+======================+
| 0                       | 16                   |
+-------------------------+----------------------+
| 2                       | 8                    |
+-------------------------+----------------------+
| 4                       | 4                    |
+-------------------------+----------------------+

What is the half-life of the compound?

A.  1 h
B.  2 h
C.  4 h
D.  6 h

Answer: B

Explanation: The level halves every 2 h.
