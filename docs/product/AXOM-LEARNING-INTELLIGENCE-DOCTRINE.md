# AXOM Learning Intelligence doctrine

Adopted 2026-10-04. Product doctrine, above individual Ideas manifests and feature briefs. Source: JD's doctrine correction and `AXOM_Master_Product_Blueprint_2026-10-04.pdf`, read in full (101 pages). The private reference PDF is not a distributed app asset.

AXOM connects what was taught, what was tested, how the learner performed, and which intervention helped. Its central question is: **What matters here, what does this learner know, what explains the gap, and what is the smallest useful next intervention?** (Blueprint pp6–8.)

## Priority hierarchy

1. Learning intelligence: understand, retain, discriminate and retrieve.
2. Assessment intelligence: how knowledge becomes a tested decision.
3. Curriculum intelligence: where it is taught and how it develops.
4. Memory and retention intelligence: delayed retrieval and transfer.
5. Personalized reasoning analysis: why this learner misses or hesitates.
6. Prioritization: what deserves attention next and why.
7. Execution support: tools for acting on that decision.
8. Productivity instrumentation: time, sessions, habits and energy as context.

Productivity remains part of AXOM. It does not define success. Judge a feature by whether it improves what the learner understands, retains, recognizes, prioritizes or retrieves. Reports lead with learning change; hours and streaks explain that change underneath. Do not claim that less study time automatically means better learning. (pp7,37–40,91,101.)

## The canonical loop

Source material → curriculum structure → assessment material → question analysis → pattern discovery → personal performance → knowledge/retrieval model → prioritization → targeted study → reassessment → updated model.

The three fingerprints are curriculum, assessment and learner. A useful recommendation connects all three when evidence exists; missing evidence stays visible. AXOM advises and the learner decides. Questions first can be a saved preference or a reasoned recommendation, never a universal learning ideology. (pp7,9,12–15,34–35,88.)

## Decode: whole term, reversible focus

Decode follows term → module → week → learning unit → concept → question cluster → question → learner error → repair. One shared academic scope coordinates Course Tracker, Question Bank, Tutor, Reports, Resources and recommendations. A breadcrumb makes focus reversible. Journal and general life surfaces retain their own context. (pp12–17.)

The Context Envelope retains relevant prerequisites, cross-module contrasts and cumulative applications outside the selected scope. Focusing a module must not erase the rest of the term. Weeks are distinct, numerically ordered containers; lectures have primary weight, DLAs secondary weight, and scheduled requirements compact weight. (pp14,16–17,23.)

**Cumulative Assessment Atlas** is the neutral product name for study across selected terms and modules. Preserve the user's exact exam label: CBSE and BSCE are not automatically interchangeable. Index concepts, rules, images, formulas and personal errors rather than requiring the learner to navigate source-file order. (pp30–31.)

## Expand for learning, compress for retrieval

Tutor is a source-grounded review interface:

Question → answer → why right → why others wrong → generative rule → source lecture → slide/image → related questions → personal error → repair.

FULL, HIGH-YIELD, PRESENTER, HANDOUT, VISUAL and CALCULATION are views over the same content and evidence. Repair joins that content to a specific learner error. A hook supplements a mechanism; it does not replace it. The user's review documents inform this information structure, not a requirement to copy their aesthetic. (pp26–29,63–64,78–79.)

Active testing does not expose answer-revealing analysis. Hints advance progressively under the chosen profile; full review follows commitment or explicit reveal. A distractor explanation states what clue would make it fit, why it fails here and which confusion it tests. (pp20,29,78.)

Pattern Maps compress multiple presentations into supported rules and contrast pairs. The Page selects those rules, personal traps, images, formulas and unresolved conflicts for one readable exam review. Every claim opens its evidence. Surface the saved page on the calendar day before a confirmed exam in the learner's timezone, without automatically buying a new AI generation. (pp21–22,36,61.)

## Truth, evidence and uncertainty

- Source questions, answer keys, explanations, titles and schedules retain their original identity. AI and learner annotations are overlays, not silent replacements.
- Code counts questions, deduplicated evidence, attempts, progress and timing. Models interpret concepts, causal rules, clues, distractors and candidate mappings.
- Every meaningful finding has source references, sample size, review state, confidence and source revision. Changed inputs mark findings stale.
- Exact copies do not multiply evidence; likely paraphrases enter review. Unresolved keys remain visible and are excluded from correctness-based recommendations.
- Separate assessment, curriculum, foundational, personal, board and cumulative high-yield. Do not call historical recurrence a prediction of the next examination.
- “Not represented in available questions” means a coverage gap, never “not testable.”
- Correctness alone is not mastery. Delayed retrieval, discrimination and transfer provide distinct evidence. Slow answers require repeated context-aware evidence before a recommendation.
- Priority is an inspectable, versioned heuristic with reasons and overrides, not a scientifically validated score.

These rules apply to imports, Tutor, Pattern Maps, reports, generated study material and The Page. (pp9,18–22,32–38,51–54,80–84,93–98.)

## Ownership, privacy and provenance

Local practice and manual organization remain useful without an AI provider or cloud account. Persist source relationships and reviewed analysis durably; include supported records in export/recovery. Binaries have their own storage boundary. “Synced” requires acknowledgement, not a started request. (pp50–53.)

Every source pack records attribution, source class/period, revision, rights status and distribution mode. Private Local, Private Synced when supported, Metadata Shared and Community Curated are distinct modes. Private ingestion does not grant public redistribution rights. Neutral naming does not clear underlying content. No supplied private PDF, extracted question bank or third-party image becomes a public fixture or bundled bank by default. (pp26,54,62,85,93.)

Cloud analysis is an explicit action with source scope and provider disclosure. No hidden source uploads, hidden paid calls or automatic transmission of Journal content. Generated questions are labeled and validated, never represented as university questions. Historical templates stay distinct from learner-confirmed current schedules and prominently disclose that course details may change. (pp25,49,53–54,85.)

## The surrounding product remains

Question Bank, Tutor, Course Tracker, schedule/attendance, reports, widgets, Locked In, timer, soundscapes, Journal/Life OS, resources, cards/Anki/Noji, accounts/sync, Daily Games, community, onboarding, desktop integration and cinematic identity remain in the ideas system. The hierarchy changes their order and role; it does not delete Ideas 1–6. Preserve useful shipped behavior and existing workstream ownership. (pp41–58,65–72.)

One next action leads the interface. Reasons follow, then expandable evidence, then technical detail. Use the existing [design contract](../design/DESIGN.md), designed light/dark states, accessible labels and calm reduced motion. Learning surfaces do not need constant ambient motion. (pp73–79,89–90.)

## Change control and delivery

Before planning or coding, read [Directions](../directions/README.md), the [idea index](../directions/01-ideas/INDEX.md), relevant verbatim idea files, [in-flight work](../directions/02-progress/IN-FLIGHT.md), this doctrine and the relevant feature plan. Check actual branch/worktree state and coordination ownership. Search before adding a second implementation. (pp55–56.)

Each new idea records its ID, domain, thesis/problem, user benefit, source/date, dependencies, priority, status, owner, implementation/verification links, tests and supersession relationship. Existing idea IDs and historical verification records stay intact. New implementation does not inherit old verification automatically.

The [Ideas 1–6 reconciliation and roadmap](IDEAS-RECONCILIATION-2026-10-04.md) governs scope and priority. [Decode implementation](../feature-development/2026-10-04/DECODE-IMPLEMENTATION.md) defines the executable sequence. The retained [Question-First Plan](../feature-development/2026-10-04/QUESTION-FIRST-PLAN.md) is its first implementation wedge, not AXOM's endpoint.

Shippable means durable, recoverable, understandable, integrated, accessible and tested. A component or provider adapter alone does not establish an end-to-end feature. Runtime status belongs in the implementation report and Directions, not in this enduring doctrine. (pp60–61,99–101.)
